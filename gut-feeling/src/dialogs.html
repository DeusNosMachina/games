The hospital arcade.

Explore a department, dodge its gremlins, then play its arcade challenge. Each medal stamps your discharge passport.

12 stars + a medal in all 6 arcade challenges + any level 2 medal = discharge. Improving a best medal earns only the additional stars. Replays still earn coins.

A better score matters

Bronze = 1 discharge star, silver = 2, gold = 3. Only your best medal per level counts. A bronze unlocks the next level. Spend earned coins on permanent equipment upgrades.

Health has a budget

Nutrients glow in the rooms. Carry up to 3; each restores 1 heart. Pickups stay collected, even after revisiting. A few fresh supplies appear at 6 and 12 stars. No unlimited snack fountain.

At zero hearts, finish Drip or Die to leave bed. Its three phases are precision timing, nutrient filtering, and pressure balance. A failed recovery goes spectacularly anatomical. Progress survives.
