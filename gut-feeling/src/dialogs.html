Medals matter

Bronze is 1 discharge star, silver 2, gold 3. Only your best medal per level counts. A bronze unlocks the next level. Spend coins in the gift shop.

Health has a budget

Nutrients glow in the rooms. Carry up to 3 (5 with Pocket snacks); each restores a heart. Pickups stay collected. A few fresh ones appear at 6 and 12 stars. No bottomless snack fountain.

Touch controls

At zero hearts, finish Drip or Die to get out of bed. Its three stages are timing, filtering and pressure. A failed recovery gets spectacularly anatomical. Progress survives.
