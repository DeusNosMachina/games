How to play

A patient’s guide to causing trouble.

Keyboard

WASD / arrows: move
Space: attack with what you’re holding
Shift / J: jump over low attacks
Q: dash through trouble
E: spin / shield / trap
1–3: equip · C: next weapon
F: eat a nutrient
M: map · Enter: nearby arcade
Esc: pause

Tap a gremlin: go bonk it. Hold to keep swinging
Flick: dash that way
Two-finger tap: jump
Long-press the floor: spin / shield / trap
Weapon strip: tap a weapon · EAT: one nutrient, one heart
Tap the glowing beacon: play

Medals matter

Bronze is 1 discharge star, silver 2, gold 3. Only your best medal per level counts. A bronze unlocks the next level. Spend coins in the gift shop.

Health has a budget

Nutrients glow in the rooms. Carry up to 3 (5 with Pocket snacks); each restores a heart. A few fresh ones appear at 6 and 12 stars. No bottomless snack fountain.

Zero hearts

Macy goes back on the IV. Finish one IV phase to get out of bed. Each collapse makes the next one harder, and a failed recovery gets spectacularly anatomical. Progress survives.
